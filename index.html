Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-02-23

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

  • Account information (e.g., username, email if provided)
  • Step count data (from Apple Health / HealthKit, with your permission)
  • App diagnostics (e.g., crash logs) if enabled on your device

Health Data

We use your step count from Apple Health to power app features such as progress tracking and leaderboards. We do not access workouts, medical records, heart rate, or other health categories unless explicitly stated.

Health data is never sold and is never used for advertising, marketing, or profiling.

How We Use Data

  • Provide core app features (step tracking, rankings, sync)
  • Maintain and improve reliability (bug fixes, security)

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may use service providers (e.g., hosting/database) only to operate the app.

Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account in the app, which deletes your associated data.

Your Rights

Depending on your location (e.g., EU/UK, California), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data.
